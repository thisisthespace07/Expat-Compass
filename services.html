What We Do

Four ways we show up for you

From spotting patterns across the community to connecting you with the right person, here's how ExpatCompass works.

01

Survey & Awareness Service

Collects real experiences and data from the foreigner community, then turns those findings into content that spreads awareness beyond ExpatCompass itself.

  • ●Community surveys — ongoing collection of real cases and challenges from foreigners across Korea.
  • ●Awareness content — findings shaped into podcasts, events, reports, or social content that reach a wider audience.
  • ●Feedback loop — what people share becomes what others learn from, building our public voice and credibility.
02

Basic Guidance & Counselling

Simple, practical direction on common situations — without acting as a law firm or caseworker.

  • ●Visa & status guidance — informed direction on options like staying in Korea after graduation, visa transitions, or renewals.
  • ●Peer-informed answers — guidance shaped by what others in similar situations have actually done.
  • ●Clear next steps — pointing you toward what to look into or do next, not just sympathy.
03

Referral & Connection Service

Connects you directly to trusted contacts when your situation needs more than guidance.

  • ●Vetted network — trusted contacts in hospitals, visa/immigration specialists, lawyers, and housing help.
  • ●Direct connections — warm handoffs to the right person, not just a generic list.
  • ●Bridge, not endpoint — we get you to the right help rather than trying to be it.
04

Community Building & Workshops

Ongoing touchpoints that bring people together and equip them with what they need — before problems even arise, not just after.

  • ●Workshops & info sessions — visa renewal clinics, "know your rights" briefings, practical how-tos — in person or online.
  • ●Onboarding packets — a starter guide for newly arrived foreigners covering visa, housing, healthcare, and banking basics.
  • ●Community meetups — casual touchpoints where survey participants and people we've helped can reconnect and become contributors themselves.
