What We Do

Four ways we show up for you

From spotting patterns across the community to connecting you with the right person, here's how ExpatCompass works.

01

Survey & Awareness Service

Collects real experiences and data from the foreigner community, then turns those findings into content that spreads awareness beyond ExpatCompass itself.

Community surveys
Ongoing collection of real cases and challenges from foreigners across Korea.
Awareness content
Findings shaped into podcasts, events, reports, or social content that reach a wider audience.
Feedback loop
What people share becomes what others learn from, building our public voice and credibility.
02

Basic Guidance & Counselling

Simple, practical direction on common situations — without acting as a law firm or caseworker.

Visa & status guidance
Informed direction on options like staying in Korea after graduation, visa transitions, or renewals.
Peer-informed answers
Guidance shaped by what others in similar situations have actually done.
Clear next steps
Pointing you toward what to look into or do next, not just sympathy.
03

Referral & Connection Service

Connects you directly to trusted contacts when your situation needs more than guidance.

Vetted network
Trusted contacts in hospitals, visa/immigration specialists, lawyers, and housing help.
Direct connections
Warm handoffs to the right person, not just a generic list.
Bridge, not endpoint
We get you to the right help rather than trying to be it.
04

Community Building & Workshops

Ongoing touchpoints that bring people together and equip them with what they need — before problems even arise, not just after.

Workshops & info sessions
Visa renewal clinics, "know your rights" briefings, practical how-tos — in person or online.
Onboarding packets
A starter guide for newly arrived foreigners covering visa, housing, healthcare, and banking basics.
Community meetups
Casual touchpoints where survey participants and people we've helped can reconnect and become contributors themselves.
