FAQ

Good to know

Is ExpatCompass a law firm or a government service? +

No. We're a knowledge base and discussion space built by and for the foreign community in Korea — not a law firm, advocacy group, or government body.

Where does the information come from? +

From the community itself — foreigners who've been through a visa dispute, a workplace conflict, or a housing headache — supplemented with vetted legal and procedural information, plus ongoing surveys and interviews across Korea.

What if my situation needs a lawyer or specialist? +

We connect you to our vetted referral network — trusted contacts in hospitals, immigration, legal, and housing — as a bridge, not an endpoint.

Can I share my own experience? +

Yes — that's how this gets built. Reach out through the contact page to share your story or join a survey.
