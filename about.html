About Us

Who We Are

So much of what you need to know as a foreigner living in Korea — how to handle a visa renewal, what to do if an employer withholds pay, how labor law actually applies to you — is scattered across outdated blog posts, hearsay in group chats, or buried in Korean-only government pages. ExpatCompass brings it into one place.

Our content is shaped by the community itself. Foreigners who've already been through a visa dispute, a workplace conflict, or a housing headache share what they learned — what worked and what didn't. We supplement these firsthand accounts with vetted legal and procedural information, so you get clear, practical answers, not just sympathy.

We don't just wait for people to come to us. We actively reach out to foreign communities across Korea — through surveys, interviews, and on-the-ground conversations — to collect real cases and experiences, and to catch gaps early as visa policies, labor conditions, and the expat community change over time.

Not a law firm. Not an advocacy group. Not the government.

We are a knowledge base and discussion space, built by and for the foreign community in Korea. When a situation needs more than guidance, we connect you to our vetted referral network instead.

Vision & Mission

Where we're headed, and how we get there

Vision

To deeply understand the real challenges foreigners face living in Korea — from visas to workplace rights to daily life — and use that understanding to build the definitive one-stop platform where any foreigner can find clear, reliable, and relevant information.

Mission

To create an open, community-driven platform where foreigners in Korea can share experiences, ask questions, and access clear, practical information on visas, employment rights, and daily life — evolving alongside the real, changing needs of the expat community.
